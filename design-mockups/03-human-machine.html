03 / FAQ

Plain answers.

Do I need to know how to code?[−]

No. Human entrants play through a normal poker interface. Coding is only required if you choose to enter a bot.

What does the bot receive?[+]

Public table state, its own cards, the decision deadline, and the exact actions it may legally return.

Is this for real money?[+]

No. It is a cooperative, play-money ACM event.

Can a failed bot stop the table?[+]

No. A failed response becomes a check when legal, otherwise a fold, and play continues.
