FAQ

Before you
take a seat.

Do I need to build a bot?−

No. You can register as a human player and play through the tournament interface.

Is real money involved?+

No. This is a friendly, play-money ACM club event.

How does a bot connect?+

Your bot hosts a small HTTP API on the event Wi-Fi. We provide the request and response schemas.

What poker format are we playing?+

No-limit Texas Hold’em, six players per table, freezeout format.
