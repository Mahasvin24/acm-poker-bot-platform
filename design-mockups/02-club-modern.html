01Can I play without coding?−

Absolutely. Register as a human and play every decision in the browser.

02Is there real money?+

No. The tournament is entirely play-money and organized as an ACM club event.

03What does my bot need?+

A small HTTP API running on your laptop and connected to the event Wi-Fi.

04What happens if my bot fails?+

The game keeps moving: it checks when possible and otherwise folds.
